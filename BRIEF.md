# BRIEF - Contemporary Pakistani Editorial

Interviewed, not self-authored. Answers are the user's choices, verbatim where given.

## The eight questions

1. **Vibe.** Contemporary Pakistani editorial. Magazine discipline, not ornament.
   From the repo: paper, ink, oxblood, antique gold; Cardo serif over DM Mono.
2. **The journey.** Fixed by the invitation's content and not negotiable:
   announcement, story, venue, programme, dress, mehendi, countdown, reply.
3. **Energy curve.** Quiet throughout. Premium-minimal: motion felt, not watched.
4. **Feeling curve and the peak.** Below.
5. **The one thing no other site does.** The invitation folds open as you scroll.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal.** Premium-minimal. Very few elements,
   enormous space.
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** One unbroken Karachi evening. The ground
   moves with the hour, 4:00 through to the last song.
8. **Assets.** Two usable photographs (courtyard, nikkah details), the shared
   motif kit, one raga recording. Six face photographs exist and are barred by
   the no-faces rule; they are parked, not used. No generation approved yet.

## The feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Stillness | Afternoon light, the names, nothing else moving |
| Announcement | Anticipation | The page goes quiet and holds for the date |
| **Date reveal** | **Discovery (PEAK)** | **The guest uncovers 17 . 10 . 26 with their own finger** |
| Story | Warmth | Three frames, light beginning to fall |
| Venue | Arrival | The courtyard opens, dusk |
| Programme | Passage | The evening runs 4:00 to 9:00, dark closing in |
| Dress, mehendi | Colour | Oxblood against the dark |
| Countdown | Weight | Night. The number is the only bright thing |
| Reply | Resolution | The page stops moving and asks |

## The peak

**The date reveal.** The sentence a guest says to a friend: *"You have to
scratch the card yourself to find out when it is."* It lives in the
announcement act, gets the most scroll room on the page, and the act before it
is deliberately silent.

## Tell-someone sentence

It is the site where the invitation folds open as you read it, and you uncover
the date with your own finger.

## Authored silence

The span between the announcement heading and the date reveal is intentionally
empty. It is the run-up to the peak, not dead scroll.
